About

A former recruiter, now on your side of the table.

I started on the hiring side, sourcing, screening, and sitting across from candidates as a recruiter. That's where I learned what actually gets a resume noticed, and what gets it passed over in six seconds.

Today I write full-time: resumes, cover letters, and LinkedIn profiles for professionals moving up, moving over, or moving into something new. I've worked with federal employees applying at GS-7 through GS-20, executives, tradespeople, nurses, and new graduates. The throughline is always the same: your experience is worth more than your current resume is saying.

Every document is built around applicant tracking systems and human reviewers at the same time, so it clears the software and still reads like it was written by someone who understands your field.
